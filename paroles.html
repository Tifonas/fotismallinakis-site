Paroles
Role: visual identity
2026

Paroles is the identity of an independent French teacher who guides adult learners toward speaking with ease. In the wordmark, a speech bubble takes the place of the O, so the name holds two meanings at once: “words”, and “you speak”.

The bubble stretches and narrows as the distance between silence and fluency closes.

A strict grid, borrowed from the student’s workbook, gives every page its order.
